Monoï, Coconut & Shea Tanning Oil

Huile Bronzante — With Monoï, Coconut & Shea Oils

200 ml

A golden tan, wrapped in monoï.

Monoï gives this oil its signature, a warm floral scent that stays with the skin long after the beach. Coconut and shea nourish beneath it, so the tan develops evenly and the skin stays soft throughout.

SPF 10 UVA/UVB Vitamin E Paraben Free
Ask About This Product

How to use

Exfoliate before you tan for an even, longer-lasting result. Apply to clean, dry skin. Reapply every 2 hours, and after every swim or workout. For adults and teens 16+.

A note on SPF 10

SPF 10 is low protection, made for gentle sun and short exposure. For midday sun, long beach days or sensitive skin, use Karicoco SPF 50+ Sunscreen Spray.

Where to find it

Available in supermarkets across Côte d’Ivoire and selected international markets.
