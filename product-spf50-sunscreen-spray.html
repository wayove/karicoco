Ask About This Product

How to use

Apply generously to clean, dry skin 15–20 minutes before going out. Using too little reduces the protection. Reapply every 2 hours, and after swimming, heavy sweating or towel-drying.

Sun safety

No sunscreen blocks all UV. Avoid direct sun between 11am and 3pm and cover up where you can. Not for use on babies under six months — keep infants and young children out of direct sunlight rather than relying on sunscreen alone. Avoid the eye area.

Where to find it
