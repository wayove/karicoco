Partner With Us

Karicoco is made in Abidjan and distributed across Côte d’Ivoire and international markets. We’re expanding, selectively.

Who Makes It

Karicoco is made by COPACI — Cosmétiques et Parfums de Côte d’Ivoire, an Abidjan manufacturer of cosmetics and fragrances since 1990. The range is stocked in supermarkets across Côte d’Ivoire, and the mentholated line has been a household product here for years.

Supply comes direct from the plant. The people who answer your questions on formulation, compliance or lead times are the people who make the product.

Two Ways to Work With Us

Stock Karicoco

For shops, supermarkets, pharmacies and salons. Order the range on wholesale terms and resupply as it moves.

Distribute Karicoco

Import and place the full range across a country or region, on territory terms.

For Distributors

We work with a small number of partners per market, and we’d rather place the brand well than place it widely.

That usually means someone who already moves personal care or FMCG in their territory, understands local cosmetic registration, and has the retail relationships to put Karicoco in front of the right shopper. Existing beauty distribution is useful. Import licences in place are more useful.

Where We Are

Karicoco is distributed across Côte d’Ivoire and in selected international markets. If your market isn’t listed, that’s usually the point.
